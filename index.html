THE VINNY WELCOME STANDARD

Hospitality should be visible.

Our inclusion methodology looks at the things guests can actually observe and experience.

01

Public signals

Policies, staff language, event programming, community participation, and visible indicators of welcome.

02

Guest experience

How people are treated, how concerns are resolved, and whether inclusion shows up consistently in hospitality.

03

Evidence over vibes

Scores should be explainable, sourced, and revisited when the evidence changes.
